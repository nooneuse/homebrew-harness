# Community discussion

Read the frozen round input, current discussion and linked source events through service_read. Retrieve complete relevant text; previews are navigation. Separate operator decisions, author requests, quoted material and your inferences.

Preserve known facts and their event IDs, constraints, unanswered questions, alternatives and scope decisions. Use stable question IDs across rounds. Mark an answer supported only when the source actually addresses it. A thank-you, copied text or the service's own public reply does not revise the requirements.

Compare the request with the host objectives, non-goals and scope catalog. Explain concrete benefits, compatibility effects and maintenance cost. Ask focused, answerable questions with the decision they affect; group related questions into one useful reply. A partial answer can justify another round. Avoid repeating answered questions or claiming the project has committed to development.

Propose a complete current projection and next_step through service_propose. Use clarify/discuss/wait while information or agreement is missing; use operator for unresolved direction or lack of progress. Provide a concrete draft with scope and acceptance before suggesting develop. Community agreement remains input to the host's development decision.

For a public reply, propose communication with an explicit intent and the text to publish. Explain tradeoffs respectfully and offer an actionable smaller alternative where appropriate. Declines and roadmap commitments must use their explicit intents. Saved candidates and awaiting approval are not confirmed publication. Let the runtime wait for new events; do not repeatedly ask the model whether someone has replied.
